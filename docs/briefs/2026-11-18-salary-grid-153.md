# Brief — The grid of the law in force (SALARY-GRID-153)

Date: 2026-11-18. Author: Opus. Builder: Sonnet. Backlog: SALARY-GRID-153 (under SALARY-R2). ADR: `docs/decisions/0008-salary-two-baselines.md`.

## Goal

Each posting gets its salary estimate from **Legea 153/2017 as it applies in 2026**. The draft-law estimate stays, as a second figure. The list, the sort order, the filter and the chart then show the pay of today, with no change to their code.

## Decisions (owner and Opus, 2026-11-18)

1. **Method (b) of S1.**
   - Anexele III, IV, VII, VIII, Anexa I Cap. II and Anexa V Cap. VI: the 2022 value × **1.15**. The two raises add up, because both count from the December 2023 level: +5% from January 2024 (OUG 115/2023, art. I alin. (1)) and +10% in June and September 2024 (OUG 53/2024, art. I alin. (1)). OUG 53/2024 art. III alin. (3) stops the +5% only for the staff of the county environmental protection agencies (art. III alin. (1)). *(Corrected 2026-11-18 by Opus. S1 used 1.05 because it did not read OUG 53/2024. The 1.15 matches ONRC and the research institute exactly.)* Before you build, confirm the factor with the lists (step 0).
   - Health and social assistance: the June 2024 column of OUG 19/2024, Anexa 1 and 2.
   - Education: the June 2024 column of OUG 128/2023.
   - Then the gradation (Art. 10) and the floor at the minimum gross wage of the date.
   - The freezes of 2025 and 2026 add nothing.
2. **Local administration is a range.** `lei_min` is the value of method (b). `lei_max` is the cap of Art. 11 alin. (4): the Anexa IX lit. C coefficient × 2,415 lei, for the population band of the UAT. Set `incredere` to `"interval"`. `motiv` says that the council sets the pay up to the cap. Where Anexa VIII has no lei value for a local post, use the territorial table as the lower end, as S1 did, and add a warning.
3. **The source is the consolidated HTML**, reviewed by hand. Save the HTML of Legea 153/2017 (legislatie.just.ro, id 298840) and of OUG 19/2024 (id 279777) in `docs/salarii/lege-153/`, with the date of download in the file name. S1 has copies in `tmp/261118/s1/d298840.html` and `d279777.html`. If you use those copies, check that they are complete. Parse only the annexes and tables that the postings use. Each grid row records its source table and row. Rows that the parser cannot read with confidence get `needs_review = 1`. List them in the report: Opus reviews them with the owner.
4. **Hospital type comes from a classifier.** Add a function in `estimate-salaries.py` that takes the employer's name and returns `a.1` (clinical: for example "Clinic", "Universitar", "Institutul") or `a.4` (other). Use `a.4` when you are not sure. Put the rule in one place. Report the split, and 20 names from each class.
5. **One JSON format, two baselines.** `salary_estimate` keeps the primary figure at the top level, in the keys of today, and adds `"baza": "lege_in_vigoare"`. The draft estimate goes under the key `"proiect"`, with `"baza": "proiect"`. Precedence (ADR 0008, rule 4): when the law in force has no row for the posting, the draft figure goes to the top level, with `"baza": "proiect"`. A row with no `baza` is an old row, and its figure is a draft figure. So `_salary_columns()`, `sal_min`, `sal_max`, `SALARY_EXPR` and the detail page continue to work with no change.
6. **A new disclaimer for the law in force:** "Estimare după Legea 153/2017 și actele care au menținut salariile în 2026; nu este un calcul salarial oficial."
7. **Known gaps: accept and record them, do not fix them.**
   - ONRC and a research institute pay 1.15 × the annex value.
   - One hospital pays workers 14–28% above (b).
   - New hires: S1 assumes the rule of OUG 115/2023 art. I alin. (5).

## Steps

0. **Confirm the factor.** Re-run the logic of `tmp/261118/s1/gap.py` with the factors 1.05, 1.10 and 1.15, for the rows that are not local and for the local rows separately. Report the median gap and the count within ±10% for each factor. Use 1.15 for the posts that are not local, unless the lists clearly reject it. For the lower end of the local range, use the factor that fits the 45 local rows best, and report it. Stop and ask only if no factor fits the posts that are not local within a median of ±5%.
1. **Parameters (S1-PARAMS, S1-ACTS).** In `data/salarii/parametri.json`:
   - Add the variant `lege153-2026` with `status_juridic: "în vigoare"`, `valoare_referinta_lei: 2415` (the reference value of Anexa IX), and `are_grila: true`.
   - Add the minimum gross wage by date: 4,050 lei from 2025-01-01.
   - Find the HG that sets 4,325 lei from 2026-07-01. If you find it, add the value with its act. If not, record the value as "unconfirmed" and use it only after the owner confirms it.
   - Correct the article numbers that S1 found (CFP is Art. 15; the ceiling of sporuri is Art. 25 alin. (1), at 30%).
   - OUG 53/2024 art. II lets the small UATs (Anexa IX lit. C poz. 23–28) pay up to 10% over the cap. Ignore it in the cap, and record it as a known gap.
   - OUG 128/2023 art. III (+8% and +12% for other staff of education units): ignore it, and record it as a known gap.
2. **The grid.**
   - `build-salary-grid.py` gets a second source (for example `--source lege-153`). It writes `data/salarii/grila-lege153-2026.csv` with the columns of today, plus `lei_min`, `lei_max`, `act_sursa`, `tip_unitate` and `multiplicator_regula`.
   - It also writes `data/salarii/plafon-local.csv`: one row for each population band, with the coefficient, the cap in lei, and the source row.
   - `--report` works for both sources.
3. **The calculator.**
   - `load_grid("lege153-2026")` loads the new grid.
   - `estimate()` computes `ceil(lei_value × multiplicator_regula × gradation multiplier)`, with the minimum-wage floor, for a row that has a lei value. A row of the draft grid continues to compute coefficient × reference value.
   - Rule 2 (the local range) and rule 4 (`tip_unitate` in the selector) go in `salary_grid.py`.
   - Do not change the result of the draft variant: the existing tests must pass with no change.
4. **The selector map.**
   - The occupation dictionary stores selectors for the draft grid. Map them to rows of the new grid in `salary_grid.py`, which is the single source of truth. Do not re-run the LLM.
   - Measure the coverage: of the postings that have a draft estimate, how many get an estimate from the law in force? Give the count per annex.
5. **The estimator.** `estimate-salaries.py` computes both baselines and writes the JSON of rule 5. `--dry-run` prints the counts per `baza` and per `incredere`.
6. **Tests.** Extend `webapp/tests/test_salary_grid.py`:
   - Golden values: the 14 functions of S1 § 4.2 at gradation 0 and gradation 5.
   - The local range for two population bands.
   - The minimum-wage floor.
   - The hospital classifier.
   - The precedence of rule 5, and the old row with no `baza`.
   Run `test_salary_facet_sql.py` too.
7. **The texts.** Three texts say "grila de salarizare propusă": `partials/salary_chart.php:48`, `partials/result_list.php:52` and `partials/facets.php:225`. Change each one to name the law in force, for example: "Salariul anunțat sau, pentru cele mai multe posturi, cel estimat după legea în vigoare (Legea 153/2017), la gradația 0." For a local post the list row shows a range: check that `list_salary()` shows it well. If it does not, report it and do not change it. The detail page with two figures is the next brief (SALARY-DETAIL).
8. **Measure.**
   - Run `python pipeline.py --steps salary,export-sqlite --force` on the local database.
   - Report the count of each `salary_bucket` value before and after (ADR 0008, rule 7).
   - Report the median change of `sal_min` per annex, and for local administration separately.
   - Re-run `tmp/261118/s1/gap.py`, or its logic, against the new grid. The S1 gap table must come out the same.
