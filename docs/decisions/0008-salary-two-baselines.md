# 0008 — Salary: two baselines, the law in force first

Date: 2026-11-18. Status: **proposed**. The owner chose rules 1–5 on 2026-11-18, after spikes S1 and S2 (`docs/audits/2026-11-18-salary-s1-current-law.md`, `docs/audits/2026-11-18-salary-s2-transparency-lists.md`). The owner accepted rule 7 on 2026-11-18 and rule 8 on 2026-11-19. The owner changed rules 2 and 4 on 2026-11-20. Rule 6 is not yet confirmed. The owner accepted rule 9 (declared pay) and its changes to rules 1, 3, 4 and 6 on 2026-11-20, after `docs/audits/2026-11-20-salary-declaratii-kibana.md`. The owner accepted rule 11 (gradation range and food allowance) on 2026-11-23, after `docs/audits/2026-11-23-salary-law-research.md`.

## Context

The site estimates the gross pay of each posting from the **July 2026 draft salary law** (`salary_grid.py`, variant `2026-07-17`, VR 4,100, gradation 0). On 2026-11-18 the owner confirmed that the draft **is not adopted**. Legea 153/2017 is still the law in force. Press reports say that the new law takes effect in 2027. So the main figure of the site describes pay that nobody gets today.

Pay under Legea 153/2017 is not one number from an annex:

- The annexes give the target values for 2022. Successive ordinances froze the pay at the „salariul de bază aflat în plată”.
- In local administration, each council (HCL) sets the pay. The law sets only the maximum.
- Each institution must publish its pay by function by 31 March and 30 September (Art. 33). That publication is the most exact source, but its format differs from one institution to the next.

The owner also asked about salariile.ro/compara. It has no model to copy. Each occupation shows one documented reference value: an eJobs survey average, an INS aggregate as context, or a range of steps from the 153/2017 grids. We take the pattern (a value with its source, a range, a page per occupation, "A vs B"). We do not take the method.

## Decision

1. **Two baselines.** Each estimate records `baza` (`lege_in_vigoare` or `proiect`), `versiune` and `status_juridic`. `lege_in_vigoare` is Legea 153/2017 with the 2026 pay rules. `proiect` is a variant of the draft law (today `2026-07-17`). Rule 9 adds a third value, `declaratii`.
2. **The law in force comes first.** The list row, the sort order, the salary chart and the filter use `lege_in_vigoare`. The detail page and the `/salarii/` hub also show `proiect` as a side note, labelled „Dacă proiectul noii legi ar fi adoptat: …”. *(Changed 2026-11-20 by the owner: nothing guarantees that the draft passes, so the draft figure is context, not a forecast.)*
3. **Local administration shows a range.** A council (HCL) sets the pay of a local post, up to the cap of Art. 11 alin. (4) (the vice-mayor indemnity: Anexa IX lit. C coefficient × 2,415 lei). The site shows a range: the lower end is the value of the law (method (b) of S1, with the factor of the brief of SALARY-GRID-153: S1 used 1.05, but OUG 53/2024 adds 10%), the upper end is the cap. S1 measured 45 local values: 25–93% of the cap, median 50%, none ≥ 95%. So the cap alone would overstate the pay, and the lower end alone would understate it by a median 21%. *(Changed 2026-11-18 by the owner, after S1. The first version showed only „maxim legal”.)*
4. **The order of precedence for one posting:** (a) the pay that the posting states; (a2) the declared pay of rule 9, when the posting matches a group of rule 9; (b) the grid of the law in force; (c) the draft grid, only when (b) has no row. *(Owner, 2026-11-20: (a2) is new.)* Each figure carries its source, and the page names it. A figure of (c) is a last resort for search: it keeps the posting in the filter and the sort order. The page labels it „estimare aproximativă” and names the draft in a tooltip or a note. The site does not adjust it. *(Owner, 2026-11-20. Measured on the local copy of 9 Sep 2026, not on the VPS data: 8,256 postings with both figures: the draft is a median 14% over the law; 10th–90th percentile +4% to +37%. In that copy, (c) gives the figure of 112 postings. Measure again on the VPS data.)* The transparency lists of Art. 33 are not a source for now: S2 found that a generic parser does not work (14 layouts in 14 files), and the owner dropped the manual entry for now (2026-11-18).
5. **Context is not an estimate.** The INS figures (TEMPO FOM106E, FOM106G, FOM121A) and the eJobs/Paylab figures go next to an estimate, with source and period. They never give the figure of a posting, the sort order or the filter.
6. **The switch rule.** When the new law is in the Monitorul Oficial, its grid becomes `lege_in_vigoare`. The 153/2017 grid becomes a previous version and stays in `data/salarii/`, so that old figures stay traceable. On the same day, the figures of rule 9 stop giving the figure of a posting, because the new law changes the pay. They stay as context until a new factor exists.
7. **The filter keeps its keys.** The values of `salary_bucket` do not change (`net-sub-3000` … `fara-estimare`, ADR 0001). Their meaning becomes "net pay under the figure of rule 4". A saved search or an email subscription can therefore get other postings once, on the day of the switch. The activity log records the counts per value before and after. *(Accepted by the owner, 2026-11-18. On the snapshot of 9 Sep, 528 of 1,822 active postings change their value.)*
8. **INS data is derived, not copied.** `ops/build-ins-context.py` reads `../tempo-ins-dump` and writes one small table, `data/salarii/ins-context.csv` (a few hundred rows, each with its matrix and period). The project does not copy the TEMPO files (`core-assets/docs/projects.md`). *(Accepted by the owner, 2026-11-19, with the estimator wizard: the INS context comes in now, as one line on the estimator result.)*

9. **Declared pay (accepted by the owner, 2026-11-20).** For some posts, the figure comes from the asset declarations (ANI), not from the grid.
   - **Source.** The holder's salary lines in the declarations filed in 2024 (pay of 2023), from the monitorul.ai Kibana. One pay line per declaration, one declaration per person: the rules of `ops/integritate-select.py`. The amounts are **net** (measured against the Art. 33 lists: ratio 0.62, net is 0.585 of gross).
   - **Group.** The same employer (`institutie_id`) and the same job title (`functie`), with at least **5** declarants. Only this level is tested. A wider group (for example county + type of unit + title) needs its own backtest first.
   - **Figure.** The p25, median and p75 of the group's yearly net pay, ÷ 12, × the factor from 2023 to 2026. `sal_min` = p25 and `sal_max` = p75, so the filter and the sort order use the lower figure, as today (rule 7). The page shows the median as the main number.
   - **Factor.** INS net average pay, section O: mean of the 2026 months ÷ mean of 2023 = **1.150** (FOM106D + FOM106G). It is measured on yearly averages, as the declarations are, and it includes the cuts of 2025. Re-measure it when INS publishes new months.
   - **Families.** Local, county and central administration; local police; MAI and MApN. Not: magistrates (law ×1.05 against INS ×1.15), court staff (backtest error 12 %), health and education (the INS factor overshoots by about 4.5 %; they wait for a legal ratio per title), elected officials, EU project staff.
   - **Error.** Backtest, out of sample (2022 → 2023 with the INS factor): median error 5–7 % for these families, p90 15–20 %. The page says „eroare tipică 6 %”.
   - **Local administration.** Where a group exists, its figure replaces the range of rule 3. Where none exists, rule 3 applies.
   - **Match.** A posting matches a group only when its employer maps to one `institutie_id` and its title to one `functie`. The build reports the match rate and a sample. An unmatched posting falls to (b).
   - **Privacy.** Only group figures leave the build: `institutie_id`, `functie`, `n`, p25, median, p75, the year and the factor. No names, no person key, no single declaration. The raw dump stays in `data/integritate/`, out of git and off the site.
   - **Label.** `baza: "declaratii"`, with `an_venit: 2023`, `factor`, `n` and `sursa`. The list row says „după declarații de avere”. The detail page has the explainer (`docs/specs/2026-11-20-salary-declared-explainer.md`).
   - **Data.** Like rule 8: a script derives one small table, `data/salarii/declaratii-grupuri.csv`, from the dump. The table is in git; the dump is not.
   - **Pages per institution (owner, 2026-11-20).** The groups also show per employer, independent of the postings: a section on `/angajator/{slug}` when the employer matches an institution, a page per institution `/salarii-declarate/{slug}/`, and an index `/salarii-declarate/`. The page shows only declared groups, and says in its first line that contract staff are not included (spike B: a council's civil-servant pay does not predict its contract-staff pay). No comparison with other employers for now.
10. **Military, police and intelligence pay, Anexa VI (owner, 2026-11-22).** The base is post pay (*soldă de funcție*) plus rank pay (*soldă de grad*), in both baselines. The page shows the two parts and the total. Bonuses (food, equipment, rent, Art. 10 compensation) are not in the figure, and the page says so.
    - **Law in force, post pay.** The 2022 lei value of Anexa VI Cap. I × **1.05** (OUG 115/2023 art. I (1), from 1 Jan 2024). OUG 53/2024 does not name Anexa VI, so its +10% does not apply. Pay is frozen in 2025 and 2026.
    - **Law in force, rank pay.** The lei table of OUG 26/2024 art. XI (from 25 Mar 2024; agent de poliție 1,980 lei). It is a separate data file with its act and its date. Art. 7 (3) ties the rank pay to the minimum wage by Government decision. No later decision is known (searched 2026-11-22). When one is found, it becomes a new version of the file, and the old file stays.
    - **Draft.** `ceil((coef_funcție + coef_grad) × VR × gradation)`, with the rank table of sheet `VI_Armata` rows 71–95.
    - **Rank of a post row.** A post row names its rank; a "debutant" row takes the rank of the same name. A row that names a range of ranks gives a range: the lowest rank for the minimum, the highest for the maximum. "Plutonier adjutant șef" maps to "plutonier adjutant principal", with a warning. A row with no rank (heads of institutions) gets post pay only, with a warning.
    - **Known gap.** The institution sets the coefficient of each function by internal order, inside the limits of the law; many orders are not public. A police union quotes 4,075 lei gross for the post pay of a debutant agent; this rule gives 3,938. The difference (137 lei) is not explained. Check it against a primary source (a MAI, IGPF or ANP notice that states the pay) before the figure leaves the estimator page.

11. **Gradation range and food allowance (owner, 2026-11-23).** A figure of the law in force becomes a range from a floor gradation to gradation 5, plus the food allowance. The legal basis is `docs/audits/2026-11-23-salary-law-research.md`.
    - **Scope.** The rule applies to (a) the stated pay and (b) the grid of the law in force. It does not apply to (a2) the declared pay of rule 9: that figure is real net pay and already holds the gradation and the bonuses. It does not apply to project-hired posts, to Anexa VI (rule 10), or to a management row (that row already holds the top gradation, Art. 19 alin. (2)). The upper end of a local range (rule 3) is a cap on the total, so it gets no gradation. The draft figure (c) and the draft side note stay at gradation 0.
    - **Floor.** The required experience gives the floor: under 3 years → gradation 0; 3 → 1; 5 → 2; 10 → 3; 15 → 4; 20 → 5. Unknown experience → gradation 0. The law grants a gradation by years of work (*vechime în muncă*, Art. 10), not by years in the field. Years in the field almost always count as years of work, so the floor is a minimum, not the gradation of the person hired. The page says „de la”.
    - **Ceiling.** Gradation 5. The percentages 7.5 / 5 / 5 / 2.5 / 2.5 apply one after the other (Art. 10 alin. (6)): gradation 5 is gradation 0 × 1.2452. Rounding follows `salary_grid.row_pay()`.
    - **Stated pay.** A stated figure is the gradation 0 value: the annexes give gradation 0 values (Art. 10 alin. (3)), and postings copy them. When the text names one gradation for the figure, the figure is the value of that gradation. VPS, 2026-11-23: 4 of 157 postings with stated pay name one gradation. *A stated range (owner, 2026-11-23):* when the posting states a min and a max, the max is the high end and gets no gradation (the posting often states gradation 0 to 5 already: 14 of 19 open stated postings give a range). The low end is the min × the floor gradation, never above the max. The food test applies at each end as usual. Cost of the first version (gradations on top of a stated range): 4,544–5,855 lei became 4,891–7,638 lei, about 1,400 lei too high at the top.
    - **Food allowance.** Add 347 lei gross at each end of the range when both conditions are true: (1) the net of that end, without the allowance, is 6,000 lei or less; (2) the employer is not one whose staff get food rights under a special law (MApN, MAI, ANP and the penitentiaries, SRI, SIE, SPP, STS, ANRSPS). The net excludes the allowance and the bonuses, as the education order does (Ordin MEC 3185/2026): the law does not say this for other sectors, and we do not know the bonuses of a post. Art. 18 alin. (1) as changed by Legea 141/2025, from 1 Jan 2026.
    - **Filter and sort.** The lower end of the range, with its food allowance, is the figure of the filter, the sort and the list row. The keys of `salary_bucket` do not change (rule 7). The activity log records the counts per value before and after.
    - **Not in the figure.** Holiday vouchers (800 lei a year, only until 31 Dec 2026) and every bonus.
    - **Values.** The food amount, the 6,000 lei cap, the gradation percentages and the excluded employers live in one data file, with the act and the date of each value. A change of the law or a ruling of the Constitutional Court (file 57D/2026, not confirmed) then changes one file.

## Consequences

- Outside local administration the figures change little: for hospitals, the pay lists are +2.8% over the draft estimate (S2) and +2% over method (b) (S1). In local administration a figure becomes a wide range. This is the correct result. The draft figures stay visible as a projection.
- `salary_estimate` (JSONB) keeps the primary figure at the top level, in the format of today, with `baza`. The draft figure goes under the key `proiect`. So `sal_min`, `sal_max`, `SALARY_EXPR`, the filter and the detail page continue to read the top level, and a row in the old format (no `baza`) reads as a draft figure.
- Health pay depends on the type of unit (OUG 19/2024: a.1 clinical … a.4 other, 1.5–2% apart). The estimator classifies the hospital from the employer's name (owner, 2026-11-18).
- `salary_grid.py` stays the single source of truth for the selector vocabulary of both baselines.
- Rule 9: for administration, police and MAI posts where a group exists, the figure becomes the real pay of the same post at the same employer, brought to 2026. In local administration it replaces a wide range with a figure and a narrower band. The figure is net and includes bonuses, so it is higher than the base pay of the grid (CJ Timiș, consilier superior: about 8,600 net against a base of 13,366 gross).
- Alternative not taken (2026-11-20): the legal factor ×1.15 from Dec 2023. Cost: it needs a correction for the raises inside 2023 (about 3 %), and it misses the cuts of 2025. It predicts about ×1.18 where INS measures ×1.150.
- Alternative not taken (2026-11-20): the INS factors for health (×1.207) and education (×1.242). Cost: in the backtest they overshoot the declared pay by about 4.5 % (universities +8.9 %).
- Alternative not taken (2026-11-20): the declarations as context only (rule 5). Cost: the site keeps a figure that it knows to be further from the real pay than the declarations are.
- Alternative not taken: keep the draft as the main figure, with a stronger note. Cost: the main figure stays wrong for every reader until 2027, and the note does not fix the sort order or the filter.
- Alternative not taken: only the law in force, and add the draft when it is adopted. Cost: readers lose the projection that the press discusses now, and we must rebuild the draft path later.
- Alternative not taken (2026-11-20): adjust a draft figure of rule 4(c) by the median gap (−14%). Cost: the site shows a number that no act publishes, for 112 postings (9 Sep copy).
- Alternative not taken (2026-11-20): no figure when the law has no row. Cost: 112 postings (9 Sep copy) drop out of the salary filter, and the reader cannot find them by pay.
- Rule 10 (2026-11-22): today no posting maps to Anexa VI (the police and the army recruit outside posturi.gov.ro), so the rule serves `/estimator-salariu/` and the `/salarii/` hub. Alternatives not taken: (1) the 2022 rank table of Art. 7 (2) (agent 250 lei). Cost: it is no longer the amount paid. (2) Post pay only, with a note. Cost: the figure misses about a third of the base pay. (3) A rank range read as the lowest rank only. Cost: the figure hides that the post can be held at a higher rank.
- Rule 11 (2026-11-23): every figure of the law in force goes up: by the food allowance (347 lei) below the 6,000 lei net cap, and by the floor gradation where the posting asks for 3 years or more. VPS, 2026-11-23: of 1,616 open postings with an estimate only, about half have no known experience and keep gradation 0. Alternatives not taken: (1) gradation 1 for every post. Cost: it overstates the pay of posts open to people with no experience. (2) The food allowance only where the text names it. Cost: 6 of 1,644 open postings name it, so those 6 rank above the same posts that do not. (3) The food allowance on every post, with no cap. Cost: the figure is wrong above 6,000 lei net. (4) Holiday vouchers in the figure. Cost: they end on 31 Dec 2026, and the figure would change again in five weeks.
- Alternative not taken: new filter keys for the new meaning. Cost: each saved search and each subscription must be migrated (ADR 0001, rule 4) for a change that the reader wants anyway.
