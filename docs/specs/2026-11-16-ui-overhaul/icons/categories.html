  • Data: export of 17 Oct 2026, data/_dev/posturi-new.sqlite, table job_postings. Active postings: 1,197 (application_status in confirmed_open, unconfirmed, unknown; the rule of OPEN_STATUS_SQL).

  • Health Icons outline, healthicons 3.1.0, licence CC0 1.0. 48 px grid, filled shapes. Shown only for medical terms.
  • Each icon is drawn at 24 px and 32 px with currentColor. Faded icons are the nearest icon for a gap. The page needs no network.

Date of the page: 17 Nov 2026. Earlier comparison of icon sets: compare.html; see also docs/decisions/0006-icon-set.md and 0007-health-icons.md.
